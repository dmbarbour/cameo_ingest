- **Status:** In progress: CP1. The maintainer approved the plan, and put diffs on the roadmap.
- **Step prefix:** `LN`, so steps are `LN-01`, `LN-02` and so on.
- **Addresses:** TR-003 (`docs/reviews/trial-2026-10-06.md`), and the maintainer, 2026-10-07:
  > "it's reasonably difficult to distinguish versioned models vs. rival models with a shared root
  > based on save time or shared content. I wonder if there's anything we could do to improve
  > that. But the origin filepath is a big clue. When presenting, we could also have some group-by
  > options, e.g. based on shared filepath prefixes or shared content."

  > "If you can find a few samples online that'd be good, but it's a reasonable inference and a
  > trial check is indeed the real test. We can also try to apply this analysis for partitioning
  > bids, as you mention."

## Why

- **Choosing models is blind:** the search page's dropdown shows each model's label, a file name
  and a short id, and nothing else. A reader can't tell a model's date, place or kin.
- **Versions and rivals look alike:** ADR-0020 joins two files as versions when they share half
  their element ids (Jaccard 0.5, or 80% of the smaller). Two bids built on one customer model
  can share that much. Joined, the subjects step (ADR-0031) shows a diagram held by both once,
  as the newest one's copy, so one bidder's changes to a shared diagram would be hidden.

## What we know

- **Element ids carry their maker and their time.** Most Cameo ids have one shape,
  `_<tool version>_<hex id>_<creation time, epoch ms>_<random>_<counter>`, e.g.
  `_18_5_1_412017e_1551451291598_995181_232897` (84% of TMT's shared ids parse; others are
  UUIDs, `eee_…` and such). What the hex part identifies (a user, a machine, an installation) is
  an inference from the shape, not documented.
- **TMT and TMT-2024x, true versions,** show the pattern a version should have:

  | | Ids | Made under (hex ids) | Created |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Shared | 31,262 | 16, `baa02e2` most | 2019 to 2023 |
  | Only in the older (deleted later) | 570 | `8c80285`, `f1d0357` and others | 2017 to 2023, before the older's save |
  | Only in the newer (added later) | 1,549 | `baa02e2` and `8c80285`, both makers of the shared part | 2023 to 2026, after the older's save |

- **Rivals on a shared root** should differ: each side's additions made under hex ids the other
  lacks, in the same period, neither after the other.
- **Cameo's project id doesn't tell:** TMT and TMT-2024x have different ones (a "save as" or a
  migration), and SAF_Blank and SAF Predefined Structure, different models, share one (a
  template).
- **The origin path is a strong clue** (the maintainer): versions tend to stay in one folder's
  lineage; bids arrive in folders of their own.
- **Fingerprints keep hashes, not ids** (`fingerprint.pack`): enough for shared shares, not for
  who made what, or when.

## Design

- **Each project's ids, summarized:** for each id, a hash (as now), its hex maker (an index into
  the project's list of makers) and its creation day, or none when the id isn't Cameo-shaped.
  About 14 bytes an id: TMT's 100,000 ids, 1.4 MB.
- **Lineage of a pair** that shares ids (ADR-0020's related threshold or more), with its evidence:
  - the share of each side's ids the other has;
  - each side's own ids: how many were made under hex ids that also made the shared part or the
    other side's, and when they were made, against the other's save time and the shared part's
    newest id;
  - their paths: a common folder, or none.
- **Kinds, each with the evidence shown,** never asserted without it:
  - **a later version of X:** its own ids made by X's makers, after X was saved; X's own few, or
    older;
  - **shares a root with X:** each side's own ids, substantial, made by makers the other lacks,
    after the shared part's newest, in overlapping periods;
  - **related:** a part shared (a library, a used project);
  - **unknown:** ids that don't carry makers (fiction, old formats): ADR-0020's rule, and the
    path, as now.
- **Families become version chains:** files of one lineage of versions. Models sharing a root
  are **kin**, shown together but never merged, so each keeps its own diagrams in subjects.
- **Bids:** a root with its kin (each with its versions) is a natural partition of a corpus of
  bids: one branch per bidder. The chooser offers it as a grouping, and a selection shortcut.
- **The chooser** (TR-003R2), in place of the dropdown: a "Models: 3 of 27" button opening a
  checklist:
  - each row: name, save date, Cameo version, folder (full paths on hover), counts, `--meta`
    values, and its lineage ("newest of 3 versions", "shares a root with …");
  - group by **folder** (a tree of path prefixes, each with a checkbox), **lineage** (roots, kin,
    versions), or **none** (sortable by name or date);
  - shortcuts: all, none, newest of each, this branch; a filter over names, paths and metadata;
  - the choice filters search results and the browse pane.

## When to stop and ask

- **Few ids parse** (under half on most real models): makers and times can't carry the analysis;
  report, with what the path and save times alone can do.
- **Real versions misread as rivals** (any of LN-03's): report before changing families.
- **Fingerprints grow too large** (over 5% of a tree's size): report, with a coarser summary.
